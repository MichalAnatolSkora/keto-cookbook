# Keto Lupin Bread (TM5)

[← Back to Recipes](../../README_EN.md)

**Author**: `skill_documenting_recipe`
**Macros per Slice** (approx. 10 slices):
- **Net Carbs**: ~0.9g
- **Fat**: ~1g
- **Protein**: ~5g
- **Calories**: ~45 kcal

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :--- | :--- |
| Lupin Flour | 100 g |
| Psyllium Husk (powder/flakes) | 40 g |
| Baking Powder | 2 tsp |
| Salt (or bread seasoning) | 0.5 tsp |
| Egg Whites | 3 pcs (or 2 whole eggs) |
| Hot Water (not boiling) | 250 g |
| Apple Cider Vinegar | 2 tbsp |

> **Tip:** Apple cider vinegar is crucial – it neutralizes the specific taste of lupin and helps the dough rise.

---

## Instructions (Thermomix TM5)

### 1. Dry Ingredients
Place all dry ingredients in the mixing bowl: flour (100g), psyllium (40g), baking powder (2 tsp), and salt (0.5 tsp).
*   **Mix**: 10 sec / speed 6.

### 2. Wet Ingredients
Add egg whites (3 pcs or 2 whole), vinegar (2 tbsp), and hot water (250g).

### 3. Knead
Knead the dough.
*   **Time**: 1 min 30 sec
*   **Mode**: Knead (Interval)

### 4. Rest
Leave the dough in the bowl for approx. 10 minutes. This is crucial – the psyllium needs to absorb the liquid so the mass thickens and becomes formable.

### 5. Shaping
With wet hands, remove the dough from the bowl and shape into a small loaf. Place on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.

### 6. Baking
Place in a preheated oven.
*   **Temperature**: 170°C (340°F) (fan/convection)
*   **Time**: 50–60 minutes

### 7. Cooling
Remove the bread and let it cool completely on a wire rack before slicing.
> **Note**: Slicing keto bread while hot can ruin its texture (it may become gummy). Let it cool!
