# Keto Lupin Bread with Sunflower Seeds (TM5)

[← Back to Recipes](../../README_EN.md)

**Author**: `skill_documenting_recipe`
**Macros per Slice** (approx. 15 slices):
- **Net Carbs**: ~2g
- **Fat**: ~4g
- **Protein**: ~6g
- **Calories**: ~75 kcal

---

## Ingredients (Scaled 1.5x)

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :--- | :--- |
| Lupin Flour | 150 g |
| Psyllium Husk (powder/flakes) | 60 g |
| Sunflower Seeds (shelled) | 50 g |
| Baking Powder | 3 tsp |
| Salt (or bread seasoning) | 0.75 tsp |
| Eggs (whole) | 3 pcs (or 4-5 egg whites) |
| Hot Water (not boiling) | 375 g |
| Apple Cider Vinegar | 3 tbsp |

> **Tip:** Apple cider vinegar is crucial – it neutralizes the specific taste of lupin and helps the dough rise.

---

## Instructions (Thermomix TM5)

### 1. Dry Ingredients
Place all dry ingredients in the mixing bowl: flour (150g), psyllium (60g), sunflower seeds (50g), baking powder (3 tsp), and salt (0.75 tsp).
*   **Mix**: 10 sec / speed 6.

### 2. Wet Ingredients
Add eggs (3 pcs), vinegar (3 tbsp), and hot water (375g).

### 3. Knead
Knead the dough.
*   **Time**: 1 min 30 sec
*   **Mode**: Knead (Interval)

### 4. Rest
Leave the dough in the bowl for approx. 10 minutes. This is crucial – the psyllium needs to absorb the liquid so the mass thickens and becomes formable.

### 5. Shaping
With wet hands, remove the dough from the bowl and shape into a loaf. You can sprinkle extra seeds on top. Place on a baking sheet lined with parchment paper.

### 6. Baking
Place in a preheated oven.
*   **Temperature**: 170°C (340°F) (fan/convection)
*   **Time**: 55–65 minutes (baking time may be slightly longer due to larger volume)

### 7. Cooling
Remove the bread and let it cool completely on a wire rack before slicing.
> **Note**: Slicing keto bread while hot can ruin its texture (it may become gummy). Let it cool!
