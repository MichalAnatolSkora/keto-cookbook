# Keto Pancakes with Cream (TM5)

[← Back to Recipes](../../README_EN.md)

**Servings**: approx. 8 pancakes

**Macros per Batch (approx. 8 pcs)**:
- **Net Carbs**: ~6g (mostly from almond/coconut flour and cream)
- **Fat**: ~70g
- **Protein**: ~35g
- **Calories**: ~820 kcal
*(Macros per 1 pancake: NC: 0.75g, F: 8.7g, P: 4.3g, Kcal: 102)*

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :--- | :--- |
| Eggs (large) | 4 pcs (approx. 220 g) |
| Heavy Cream (30%) | 60 g |
| Water (sparkling preferred for fluffiness) | 60 g + optional 20 g (step 4) |
| Butter (melted and slightly cooled) | 30 g (approx. 2 tbsp) |
| Almond Flour | 60 g (approx. 4 heaping tbsp) |
| Coconut Flour | 10 g (approx. 1 level tbsp) |
| Psyllium Husk | 4 g (approx. 1 level tsp) |
| Inulin | 15 g (approx. 1.5 tbsp - gives a mild sweetness) |
| Vanilla Extract | 1 tsp (highly recommended - hides egg flavor) |
| Salt | a pinch |

> **Tip:** Heavy cream mixed with water (especially sparkling) is a perfect compromise - it gives the batter a rich, creamy flavor, while the water makes the pancakes light.

---

## Instructions (Thermomix TM5)

### 1. Mixing All Ingredients
Place all ingredients in the mixing bowl:
* **4 large eggs** (220g),
* pour in **60 g heavy cream (30%)** and **60 g water**,
* add **30 g melted butter** and **1 tsp vanilla extract**,
* add dry ingredients: **60 g almond flour**, **10 g coconut flour**, **4 g psyllium husk**, **15 g inulin** and **a pinch of salt**.

Insert the measuring cup and mix: **20 sec / speed 4**.

### 2. Scraping Down
Remove the lid and use the spatula to scrape the batter down from the sides to the bottom of the mixing bowl. 
Mix again to ensure there are no lumps: **10 sec / speed 4**.

### 3. Batter Rest (Very Important!)
Leave the batter in the mixing bowl for **10 to 15 minutes**. During this time, the psyllium and coconut flour will absorb the liquid and thicken the mixture.

### 4. Checking Consistency
After resting, stir the batter with the spatula. It should have the consistency of thick, but still pourable, cream. If it has become too thick (e.g., coconut flour absorbed a lot of water), add **20 g water** to the bowl and mix: **10 sec / speed 3**.

### 5. Frying in the Pan

1. Heat a dry pan over medium heat (keto pancakes prefer a slightly lower temperature than wheat ones).
2. Pour thin portions of batter (best distributed in a circular motion around the pan).
3. Once the batter is spread, add a little olive oil to the pan. This prevents sticking and adds crispiness.
4. Fry patiently for approx. 2-3 minutes on one side. **Don't rush to flip!** Wait until the top is completely matte and the edges start to lift away from the pan on their own.
5. Gently slide a wide spatula underneath and flip to the other side for about a minute (add a little more olive oil to the pan if needed).

Done! Thanks to the vanilla, cream, and inulin, the aroma and taste are excellent. Enjoy!
