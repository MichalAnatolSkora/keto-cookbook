# Keto Strawberry Ice Cream (TM5)

[← Back to Recipes](../../README_EN.md)

**Servings**: approx. 4 servings

**Macros per Batch**:
- **Net Carbs**: ~32g
- **Fat**: ~65g
- **Protein**: ~15g
- **Calories**: ~780 kcal
*(Macros per 1 serving: NC: 8g, F: 16g, P: 4g, Kcal: 195)*

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :--- | :--- |
| Frozen Strawberries | 450 g |
| Cream Cheese (e.g., Philadelphia) | 150 g |
| Heavy Cream (30%) | 75 g |
| Vanilla Extract | 1.5 tsp |
| Lemon juice | 1.5 tsp |
| Coconut flakes | for sprinkling |
| Keto Sweetener (e.g., erythritol) | optional, to taste (approx. 20-30 g) |

---

## Instructions (Thermomix TM5)

### 1. Chopping the strawberries
Place **450 g frozen strawberries** in the mixing bowl, pour in **1.5 tsp lemon juice** and **1.5 tsp vanilla extract**.
Chop: **15-20 sec / speed 8**.

### 2. Scraping down
Remove the lid and use the spatula to scrape the chopped strawberries down from the sides to the bottom of the mixing bowl.

### 3. Mixing the base
Add **150 g cream cheese (Philadelphia)**, **75 g heavy cream (30%)** and optionally **keto sweetener** (e.g., erythritol, approx. 20-30 g).
Mix while assisting with the spatula through the hole in the lid: **20-30 sec / speed 4**.

Done! Serve immediately in cups and sprinkle with coconut flakes. Enjoy!

> **Tip:** Because we use frozen strawberries, the ice cream is ready to eat immediately with a soft-serve consistency. If you prefer a firmer, scoopable texture or want to save it for later, simply transfer the mixture to a container and freeze for 1-2 hours.
