# Keto Cheesecake (Crustless)

[← Back to Recipes](../../README_EN.md)

**Author**: `skill_documenting_recipe`
**Macros per Serving** (approx. 12 slices):
- **Net Carbs**: ~6g
- **Fat**: ~18g
- **Protein**: ~18g
- **Calories**: ~260 kcal

---

## Ingredients

| Ingredient | Quantity |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cheesecake Curd (ground cheese) | 1 kg |
| Erythritol (powdered) | 150-200 g |
| Eggs (large) | 5 pcs |
| Butter (softened) | 100 g |
| Sugar-free Vanilla Pudding (or Coconut Flour) | 1 pack (40g) / 30g |
| Vanilla Extract | 1 tsp |

---

## Instructions

### 1. Mix Butter
Cream the softened butter (100g) with powdered erythritol (150-200g) until fluffy.

### 2. Add Eggs
Add eggs (5 pcs) one by one, mixing constantly at low speed.

### 3. Cheese Mixture
Add the cheese (1kg) in make batches, and finally add the pudding powder (1 pack / 30g flour) and vanilla (1 tsp). Mix only until combined (do not over-aerate, or the cheesecake will collapse).

### 4. Baking
Pour the mixture into a springform pan (24 cm / ~9 inch). Place in the oven (optionally with a water bath at the bottom).
*   **Temperature**: 150°C (300°F)
*   **Time**: 60–70 minutes

### 5. Cooling
After baking, leave the cheesecake in the turned-off oven with the door ajar to cool, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours (preferably overnight).

> **Tip**: Baking with a water bath (heat-proof dish with hot water on the oven floor) prevents the cheesecake from cracking.
