# Keto Carrot Cake (TM6)

[← Back to Table of Contents](../../README_EN.md)

**Author**: `skill_documenting_recipe`
**Macros per Serving** (for 12 slices):
- **Net Carbs**: ~3.5g
- **Fat**: ~26g
- **Protein**: ~7g
- **Calories**: ~280 kcal

---

## Ingredients

### Base:
| Ingredient | Amount |
| :--- | :--- |
| Carrots (peeled, in pieces) | 200 g |
| Almond flour | 200 g |
| Erythritol | 130 g |
| Butter (soft) | 80 g |
| Eggs (size L) | 3 pcs |
| Walnuts | 50 g |
| Cinnamon | 1.5 tsp |
| Baking powder | 1 tsp |
| Salt | Pinch |
| Inulin (optional, for moisture)| 1 tbsp |

### Cream:
| Ingredient | Amount |
| :--- | :--- |
| Cream cheese (Philadelphia/Mascarpone, cold)| 200 g |
| Butter (soft) | 40 g |
| Erythritol | 30 g |
| Vanilla extract | 1 tsp |

---

## Instructions (Thermomix TM6)

### 1. Preparation
Preheat the oven to **180°C**. Line a springform pan (approx. 20 cm) with baking paper.

### 2. Grinding erythritol
Pour the entire **160g of erythritol** (130g for the cake + 30g for the cream) into a clean and dry mixing bowl.
*   **Grinding**: 10 sec / speed 10
Transfer to a bowl and separate: set aside **30g** in a separate bowl for the cream, and keep the remaining **130g** for the cake.

### 3. Chopping walnuts
Add **50g of walnuts** to the mixing bowl.
*   **Chopping**: 3 sec / speed 5
Transfer to a bowl and set aside.

### 4. Chopping carrots
Add **200g of carrots** to the mixing bowl.
*   **Chopping**: 5 sec / speed 5
If the carrots are very juicy, squeeze out the excess juice gently with your hands. Leave the carrots in the mixing bowl.

### 5. Whipping eggs with erythritol
Add **3 eggs** and the previously prepared **130g of ground erythritol** to the carrots. Insert the butterfly whisk.
*   **Whipping**: 3 min / speed 4

### 6. Combining ingredients
Remove the butterfly whisk. Add **80g of soft butter**, **200g of almond flour**, **1.5 tsp of cinnamon**, **1 tsp of baking powder**, **a pinch of salt**, and optionally inulin.
*   **Mixing**: 20 sec / speed 4

### 7. Adding walnuts
Add the previously chopped **50g of walnuts**.
*   **Mixing**: 15 sec / reverse speed 3

### 8. Baking
Transfer the batter to the prepared pan.
*   **Temperature**: 180°C
*   **Time**: 40–45 minutes (until a toothpick comes out dry)
Cool completely before applying the cream.

### 9. Preparing the cream
Pour the previously set aside **30g of ground erythritol** and **40g of soft butter** into a clean and dry mixing bowl.
*   **Mixing**: 1 min / speed 3 (until a fluffy mass is formed)
Then add **200g of cold cream cheese** and **1 tsp of vanilla extract**.
*   **Mixing**: 20 sec / speed 3

### 10. Finale
Spread the cream on the cold cake. It tastes best after 2-3 hours in the fridge.

> **Tip for TM6**: You can use the "Scale" function at each step to accurately measure ingredients directly into the mixing bowl. If you find the cake too sweet after baking due to the 130g of erythritol, add a few drops of lemon juice to the cream (step 9) – it will balance the taste perfectly!
